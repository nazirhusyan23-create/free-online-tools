Free Online Tools is designed to be your go-to, all-in-one digital utility belt. Whether you are a writer checking copy length, a developer validating structured data, or someone looking to quickly generate a secure password or QR code, our platform provides instant results right in your browser.

We believe utility tools should be completely private. That is why all processing—including word counting, JSON formatting, password generation, and QR code creation—happens entirely client-side on your device. Your data is never uploaded or stored on any server.

No. All processing — counting words, formatting JSON, generating passwords and QR codes — happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I use Free Online Tools on my phone?

Yes, the whole toolbox is fully responsive and works smoothly on mobile, tablet, and desktop browsers.
